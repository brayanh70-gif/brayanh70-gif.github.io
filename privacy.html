Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the AI_MEDIA_CORP app ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Wh4tTheClip to manage that channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uploads videos and their metadata to the owner's own channel and reads that channel's statistics and analytics.
  • It reads public data about public videos and channels (titles, view counts, durations) to research topics.

How the information is used and stored

  • Data is used only to produce, publish and improve the channel's own videos.
  • Access tokens and data are stored only on the owner's own computer, protected by the operating system's credential protection. Nothing is stored on a public server.
  • The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.
  • The app does not collect information about viewers.

Cookies

The app does not place cookies or tracking technology on any device. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Stored statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for the channel and are deleted when no longer needed. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page; once revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and the owner deletes them from the computer.
